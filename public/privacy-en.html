Last Updated: October 28, 2026

Summary:After logging in, your test results will be securely saved so you can review them anytime, compare yourself at different times, and track long-term changes. If you buy or claim a full report, we also save the related order and payment records and email you a payment notice. Below is the full text for your reference.

1. What we save for your records

So that you can have your own test records and review them long-term after logging in, and so that we can complete purchases and support you, we save:

  • Login Account: The name, email, and account identifier you provide when signing in with Google, used to verify your identity and keep records for you.
  • Your Test Results: Your MBTI results and related analysis, for your later review and comparison (if you are not logged in, they will only be saved on your device and will not be uploaded).
  • Login Time and Method: Used for your usage records and account security.
  • Payment and Order Records: When you buy a full report we save the order number, the report type, the amount and currency, the payment status and time, the LINE Pay transaction number and the payment result it returns, the account identifier you were signed in with, and a system-generated payment reference number (starting with "KW-"). Payment is completed on the LINE Pay page; we do not receive your full card number, bank account details or LINE Pay password. When you claim a report during the free promotion, we save the claim record (account, report type, time and source).
  • Email: Used to send payment notices (including the payment reference number) and test-result emails.
  • Usage and Device Data: The website uses Google Analytics 4 and LINE Tag to collect usage information, such as pages viewed, test and button actions, traffic-source parameters (such as from and utm), browser and device information, approximate location, and a cookie identifier that recognises your browser.
  • Internal Notifications to the Shop: When a test is completed or a payment is confirmed, the system sends a notice to a Discord channel visible only to the shop's administrators. A test-completion notice includes the test version and result type, language, traffic source, the country/city derived from your IP address (not the IP address itself) and device/browser type; if you are signed in, it also shows your member name and email (if you are not signed in, the notice contains no name or email). A payment notice includes the report type, amount, payment reference code and payment time.

You can play the test without logging in, but there will be no cross-device records and long-term tracking functions. The above saving is based on your consent and service needs.

2. Use of this information

Used only to provide the service and for the following purposes:

  • To give you complete test results and analysis, and to review and track your changes long-term
  • To process payments, confirm and unlock the full report you bought, send payment notices and test-result emails, and handle support, refunds and disputes
  • If you have added our LINE, you may receive event or discount notifications (can be canceled at any time)
  • To analyse usage statistically, improve service quality and measure advertising performance
  • For the shop's internal awareness of completed tests and payments, so that we can provide support and run the business

3. How your records are saved and protected

  • Logins and accounts are securely managed by Supabase Auth, and your records are transmitted via HTTPS encryption.
  • We only keep it for as long as you need to "have a record and review it"; if you delete your account or request deletion, we will delete it within a reasonable period, except as otherwise provided by law.
  • Payment and order records are kept for the period required by law for accounting, tax and for handling refunds or disputes, and are used only for those purposes while kept.
  • We will not sell your personal data to anyone; we share it only with the service providers needed to deliver the service (see Section 4).

3-1. Storage Method of Records

We use cloud services such as Supabase, Google, Cloudinary, LINE Pay, Resend, Discord and LINE to save your login and records, process payments and send notices. These services may operate outside the country, and we will choose service providers with security standards. Using this service indicates that you understand and agree to this arrangement.

Login, payment, notices and statistics are handled through the following services, each with its own privacy explanation:

  • Supabase: For user authentication and account session management Privacy Policy

5. Cookies

We use Cookies to remember your login status, so you don’t have to log in again next time you want to review your records. Google Analytics 4 and LINE Tag also use Cookies or similar technologies to recognise your browser for usage statistics and advertising measurement; you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's opt-out browser add-on. You can disable Cookies in your browser settings, but you may not be able to use login, records and long-term tracking functions.

Your records are yours. You can inquire, correct, or delete them at any time, and you can also cancel event notifications. We will respond within the legal time limit. Payment and order records that the law requires us to keep will be retained for the legal period. We do not sell your personal data; if the laws in your region grant you other rights, we will also respect them.

To exercise your rights, please contact us via LINE or Linktree.
